Data Sources Used by the System

The Heatwave Intelligence System can use information from different weather, climate and satellite sources to monitor and predict heatwave conditions.

Weather Data

Data Type Purpose
Temperature Used to monitor changes in surface and air temperature.
Humidity Helps understand how hot conditions may feel to people.
Wind Speed Helps analyze weather conditions and heat distribution.
Rainfall Helps identify dry conditions and changes in local weather.
Weather Forecasts Used to estimate future temperature and heatwave conditions.

Satellite Data

Satellite observations can provide information about land surface temperature, vegetation and other environmental conditions over large geographical areas.

  • Land Surface Temperature
  • Vegetation Information
  • Surface Conditions
  • Geographical Information

Climate Data

Historical climate records can be analyzed to understand temperature patterns and identify unusual periods of extreme heat.

Comparing current observations with historical data can help determine whether temperatures are significantly higher than normal.

Data Processing

  1. Collect weather and climate observations.
  2. Clean and organize the collected data.
  3. Analyze temperature and environmental conditions.
  4. Compare current conditions with historical patterns.
  5. Use the results for heatwave monitoring and prediction.

Importance of Reliable Data

Accurate and timely data is important for detecting heatwave conditions and providing useful early warnings. Reliable data can help authorities and the public make better decisions during extreme heat events.
