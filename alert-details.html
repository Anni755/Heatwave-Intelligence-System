Current Heatwave Alerts

This page provides information about heatwave alerts identified by the Heatwave Intelligence System.

Alert Levels

Alert Level Condition Recommended Action
Normal No significant heatwave conditions detected. Continue normal activities.
Watch Increasing temperature and possible heatwave conditions. Stay informed and monitor weather updates.
Warning Heatwave conditions are likely or developing. Avoid unnecessary outdoor activities.
Severe Warning Extreme heat conditions detected. Stay indoors and follow safety advisories.

Heatwave Safety Advisory

  • Drink sufficient water and stay hydrated.
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight.
  • Avoid strenuous outdoor activities during peak heat.
  • Wear lightweight and loose-fitting clothing.
  • Check on elderly people and other vulnerable individuals.

Alert Information

The system can use temperature observations, weather data and forecast information to identify areas where heatwave conditions may develop.

Always follow official local weather and emergency advisories during extreme heat events.
