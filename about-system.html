About the System

The Heatwave Intelligence System is a web-based climate monitoring platform designed to provide information about heatwave conditions.

The system combines weather observations, climate data and forecasting information to help identify areas that may experience extreme heat.

Objectives of the System

  • Monitor temperature and weather conditions.
  • Identify potential heatwave conditions.
  • Provide heatwave forecasts and alerts.
  • Identify regions affected by extreme temperatures.
  • Provide useful safety information to the public.

How the System Works

  1. Collect temperature and weather observations.
  2. Process and analyze the collected data.
  3. Identify areas showing heatwave conditions.
  4. Use forecast information to estimate future conditions.
  5. Display alerts and advisories through the website.

Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence can help analyze large amounts of weather and climate data and identify patterns associated with heatwave events.

Machine learning models can also be used to improve heatwave prediction and provide early warnings for potentially affected regions.

Who Can Use the System?

  • Citizens and the general public
  • Meteorologists
  • Disaster-management authorities
  • Researchers and students
  • Government agencies
