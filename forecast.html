How Heatwave Prediction Works

  1. Collect current temperature and weather observations.
  2. Analyze historical climate data.
  3. Study weather forecast information.
  4. Analyze geographical and environmental conditions.
  5. Use predictive models to estimate future heat conditions.
  6. Generate heatwave warnings when risk levels increase.

Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning can be used to analyze large amounts of historical and current weather data.

These models can identify patterns in temperature, humidity, wind and other environmental variables to help predict possible heatwave conditions.

Forecast Advisory

Forecast information may change as new weather observations become available. Users should check official weather updates before making decisions during extreme heat conditions.
